# MAIL — six integrated delivery gates

Decision: consolidate remaining outbound mail implementation into six acceptance gates instead of separate micro-PRs. The site uses password-based accounts; the channel remains disabled until explicit operator acceptance. CI never sends real mail, deploys to the VPS or provisions billable resources.

1. Offline foundation completion: merge accepted recipient preflight and stopped-only current-month reconciliation, with real PostgreSQL and full integration CI. Preserve monthly history and the budget guard. No refund or assumed free credit; no automatic enable after a restore. Keep the existing 100/300/400/500 RUB thresholds and 200/day, 1/sec caps unless explicitly approved otherwise.
2. Secure Postbox transport: fixed allowlisted provider endpoint; maintained MIME/TLS implementation, certificate validation, no plaintext fallback, deadlines, redacted outcomes distinguishing definitive nonacceptance from uncertainty; deterministic local mock tests. No credentials or runtime worker.
3. Operator controls: /odmen provider configuration and reconciliation UI; live administrator role and CSRF/Origin, encrypted secrets, rotation and recovery, revision-fenced settings, safe probe/test-send separated from activation. A separate activation gate must require externally verified billing/recovery evidence and an independent restore/launch fence outside the recovered database. An older backup must never silently resume sending or obtain fresh free allowance. Do not treat a digest or DB revision alone as proof that no attempts were lost.
4. Dispatch: bounded opted-in worker, durable deferred scheduling and claim fencing, trusted source/account recheck, shared recipient preflight, suppression, quota and cost reservation, one correlated submission, safe classification and recovery of uncertain work. No SMTP inside DB transactions. Check final send-start fence, cap queue/metrics cardinality and retention, respect news reserve and stop switch.
5. Product flows: one-time expiring email proof and password recovery links (no passwordless login), support reply notifications without conversation content, opt-in news with unsubscribe and source/consent rechecks, idempotent enqueue and abuse limits. Preserve existing account behavior; no forced re-registration. Password recovery must revoke affected sessions and resist account enumeration. 
6. Operational acceptance: disposable full-stack E2E, recovery of queue, costs and keys from retained backup, simulate provider/network outages and stop/start; verify monitoring, DNS identity (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), actual inbox delivery and feedback with explicitly authorized test mail. Production activation and any paid resources require separate operator decision; deploy is manual.

Every gate has one principal PR (or combined acceptance branch), required CI and, after merge, full-main acceptance. Independent offline work may proceed on a separate draft while CI runs. Do not merge superseded overlapping branches separately. Never weaken required checks for speed.
